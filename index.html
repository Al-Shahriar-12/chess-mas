About the developer
MAS Studio

MD Al Shahriar

BSc in Civil Engineering · BUET

Civil engineering graduate of the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET) and founder of MAS Studio — MAS stands for Mohammad Al Shahriar.

MAS Studio builds free, open-source web apps for civil engineering students in Bangladesh: structural design tools that run in any browser, with no installs and no paywalls. Chess.MAS is the studio's game for the hours between the textbooks.
